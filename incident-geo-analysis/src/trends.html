Why this does not match the incident browser

These are UCR/NIBRS figures: offences counted within incidents on a national standard, dated by when they were reported to the FBI. The incident browser is the city's own monthly Crime Watch report — a curated list of selected incidents, dated by when they occurred. The two use different definitions and different timing, so their totals differ, and neither is a check on the other. They are kept in separate tables and on separate pages for that reason.

The FBI's public data covers the ten categories above and no others. Weapons and drug offences are not available through it, so an incident like the explosive devices found near Wynne Park in March 2025 appears in neither source.

Source: FBI Crime Data Explorer, agency TX0571100.
